2 · METHODOLOGY

Two user studies

Experts

Microsoft’s sentiment team
5-hour session

Controlled experiment

18 users · 2 hours · BERT on QQP
3 groups: none · capabilities · full tool

Ribeiro et al. (2020), §4

To evaluate CheckList itself, the authors ran two user studies. The first targeted experts: the team behind Microsoft’s commercial sentiment model, already extensively tested. In a five-hour session, the authors presented the methodology without their own tests; the team brainstormed about 30 tests and implemented about 20. The second was a controlled experiment with eighteen participants with intermediate NLP experience, eight from industry and ten from academia, who tested BERT on QQP for two hours. They were split into three groups of six: no guidance, the list of capabilities only, and capabilities plus the templating tool. Due to time, only MFTs were written. Bug severity was rated from one to five and re-rated by an external person.
