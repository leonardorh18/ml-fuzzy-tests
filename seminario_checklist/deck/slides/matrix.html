Ribeiro et al. (2020), Figure 1

Conceptually, users “CheckList” a model by filling in a matrix. Rows are linguistic capabilities, such as vocabulary, named entities or negation; the paper also suggests taxonomy, robustness, fairness, temporal reasoning, coreference, semantic roles and logic. Columns are three test types, explained next. Each cell reports a failure rate. In this example from Figure 1, a commercial sentiment model fails 76.4% of a simple negation test. Because the model is treated as a black box, research and commercial systems can be compared directly.
