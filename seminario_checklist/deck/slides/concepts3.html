Part 2: Finally, the tasks and models. Sentiment analysis labels a text as positive, negative or neutral. Quora Question Pairs asks whether two questions are duplicates, that is, ask the same thing. Reading comprehension, on the SQuAD dataset, asks the model to answer a question using a paragraph. The models tested include BERT and RoBERTa: Transformer language models pretrained on large text corpora and then fine-tuned for each task. RoBERTa also appears as a tool: as a masked language model it predicts a hidden word, which CheckList uses to suggest words for its templates.
