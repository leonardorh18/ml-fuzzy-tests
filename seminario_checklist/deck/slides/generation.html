PART 3 · METHODOLOGY

Templates generate many tests

I {NEGATION} {POS_VERB} the {THING}.

didn’t / can’t say I · love / like · food / flight / service

Plus: RoBERTa word suggestions, lists of names and places, ready-made typos.

Ribeiro et al. (2020), §2.3

Writing tests by hand is slow. CheckList scales test creation with templates: a sentence with placeholders, each filled from a list, so that every combination becomes a test case. To fill these lists, the tool masks a word, for example “I really {mask} the flight”, and RoBERTa suggests words such as enjoyed, liked, loved or regret, which the user sorts into lexicons. The tool also provides lists of names, places and protected groups, and general perturbations such as character swaps that simulate typos. Suites can be saved and reused with other models.
