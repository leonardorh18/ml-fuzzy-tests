Ribeiro et al. (2020), Tables 2 and 3

On Quora Question Pairs, BERT and RoBERTa surpass human accuracy on the benchmark. Yet BERT confuses “before” and “after” in 98% of cases, both models ignore modifiers that change the meaning of a question, fail synonyms and antonyms, and even break symmetry: swapping the two questions changes 2 to 4% of predictions. In reading comprehension, BERT-large fails every simple coreference test, such as “He is a journalist, and she is an adviser. Who is a journalist?”. It also shows gender bias: given “John is not a doctor, Mary is”, it picks the man as the doctor 89.1% of the time, but only 3.2% when the names are reversed. The authors interpret these failures as evidence that the models rely on shortcuts, such as anchoring on named entities.
