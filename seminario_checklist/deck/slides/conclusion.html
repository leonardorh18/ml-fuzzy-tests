PART 4 · CONCLUSION

Accuracy is useful, but not enough

Takeaways

  • Works for any task and model
  • Finds bugs even in products
  • Easy to learn

Limitations

  • Behavior only (not labels, security)
  • Passing ≠ correct
  • Small user study

From “how accurate is it?” to “what can it do?”

Ribeiro et al. (2020), §5–6 · “small user study” is the presenters’ observation

Part 4: To conclude: benchmark accuracy is useful but not sufficient. A behavioral methodology, independent of model and task, revealed critical bugs both in research models that have “solved” benchmarks and in commercial products, and the user studies indicate that it is easy to learn. The authors acknowledge that CheckList does not address non-behavioral problems such as labeling errors, data versioning, worst-case security or interpretability, and that passing a test does not prove a capability. We would add that the user study is small. In one sentence: CheckList replaces the question “how accurate is the model?” with “what can the model actually do?”.
