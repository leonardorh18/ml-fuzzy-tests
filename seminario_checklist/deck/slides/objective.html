1 · INTRODUCTION

Objective

A task-agnostic method to test what an NLP model can do, not just how accurate it is.

Method

Capabilities × test types

Tool

Open-source test generator

Evidence

3 tasks + 2 user studies

Ribeiro et al. (2020), §1

The objective is to bring behavioral testing to NLP in a way that does not depend on the task or the model. The paper does not list formal research questions, but three are implicit: can one framework guide what to test across tasks; does it reveal failures in models that look solved on benchmarks; and do practitioners find more bugs with it. The contributions answer these: the methodology, an open-source tool, an evaluation on three tasks, and two user studies.
