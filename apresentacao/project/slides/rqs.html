2 · RESEARCH QUESTIONS AND MOTIVATION

Is grammar fuzzing + differential testing effective — and what limits it?

RQ1 · Effectiveness

How many failures, of which kinds, and at what cost?

RQ2 · Oracles

What do differential and metamorphic oracles add beyond a known answer?

RQ3 · Guidance

Does coverage-guided expansion beat random expansion?

RQ4 · Oracle reliability

How often do the automatic oracles misjudge a reply?

Motivation: existing LLM fuzzers target jailbreaks, and metamorphic frameworks compare a model only with itself. Nobody measured how often these oracles are right.

Quatro perguntas. RQ1 e RQ2 descrevem o que a técnica encontra; RQ3 avalia uma escolha de projeto do gerador; RQ4 mede o erro dos próprios oráculos, que sustenta todos os outros números. A motivação: fuzzers de LLM hoje miram jailbreak, e frameworks metamórficos comparam o modelo consigo mesmo — ninguém mediu quanto esses oráculos acertam.
