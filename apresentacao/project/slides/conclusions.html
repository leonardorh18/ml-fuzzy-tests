7 · CONCLUSIONS AND LIMITATIONS

Input generation was not the bottleneck — oracles were

Conclusions

  • Failures in 64.5% of replies, in every model and category
  • Known answers turn differential and metamorphic signals into verdicts
  • Paraphrases expose answers that depend on wording
  • Coverage guidance covers the grammar, not more behaviors
  • Report results under a content and a format criterion

Limitations

  • Three small local models; hosted API not tested
  • Only tasks with a computable answer are checked
  • One seed in the main campaign; 3 in Ablation 2
  • Manual judgments AI-assisted, pending review
  • English, single-turn, templated prompts
